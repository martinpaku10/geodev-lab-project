Project brief: Flood risk map of Cape Coast, Ghana
Part 1: The question
Which settlements, roads, and critical infrastructure in the Cape Coast Metropolis lie in areas classified as high or very high flood susceptibility zones?

Part 2: Why it matters
The Cape Coast Metropolis experiences escalating flood events with limited research attention despite its vulnerability, particularly around the Kakum River estuary. The Cape Coast Metropolitan Assembly and NADMO would use this map to prioritize drainage investment, restrict development in hazardous zones, and plan evacuation routes to protect lives and livelihoods.

Part 3: The data I need
Administrative boundary for Cape Coast Metropolis

Digital Elevation Model (DEM)

Watercourse network (rivers, streams, drainage)

Settlement extents and land cover

Historical flood event locations

Road network

Part 4: Where each dataset comes from
Administrative boundary – HDX Ghana – https://data.humdata.org/dataset/ghana-administrative-boundaries

Digital Elevation Model – NASA Earthdata – https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/

Watercourses – WASCAL – https://dataportal.pauwes.dz/geonetwork/srv/api/records/eedcc530-8ace-46b8-ad2c-448242dd1b88

Settlement extents – USGS Earth Explorer – https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/

Historical flood locations – NADMO Cape Coast – (no online link – contact directly)

Road network – Geofabrik – https://www.geofabrik.de/data/shapefiles.html

Part 5: What I would build
A static flood risk map and web dashboard showing susceptibility zones overlaid with settlements, roads, and critical infrastructure. Local government officers and NADMO officials could open it to identify high-risk areas for planning interventions and emergency response.
